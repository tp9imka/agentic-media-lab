# Brief: "Quote first" (20 s)

The same brief is given to an agent twice: once with the HyperFrames skills, once with the
Remotion Agent Skills. Nothing else differs.

## Deliverable

One MP4: 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, 20.0 s (600 frames), H.264, no audio track required.

## Content (use this copy exactly, do not add scenes or text)

| # | Seconds | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 - 3 | Title: "Quote first." Subtitle: "How one claim gets into the hub" |
| 2 | 3 - 8 | Step 1: "Open the primary source." Supporting line: "The skill file. The licence. The pricing page." |
| 3 | 8 - 13 | Step 2: "Copy the quote. Then write the sentence." Show a quote block containing: "Exits are 40-50% shorter than their entrances." and under it the label "motion.md, section Motion craft" |
| 4 | 13 - 17 | Step 3: "The gate finds the quote in the saved source." Show the line: "quote-found: 147   quote-not-found: 0" in a monospace face |
| 5 | 17 - 20 | Closing card: "No quote, no claim." Small line: "agentic-media-lab" |

## Look

- Background `#0F1419`, text `#F2F4F7`, one accent `#4CC38A`. No other hues. No gradients,
  no stock imagery, no logos, no emoji.
- System fonts only (a sans for copy, a monospace for scene 4). No font downloads.
- Motion is yours to design within the skill's rules. Every scene needs an entrance; the
  video needs transitions between scenes.

## Rules for the run

- Follow the installed skill. Do not consult other skills, blog posts or example repos.
- Local render only. No hosted service, no API key, no sign-in, no telemetry opt-in.
- Do everything inside this arm's folder. `npm` installs go into the folder, never global.
- No human feedback during the run. If the skill tells you to inspect frames, do it yourself.
- Stop at 30 minutes of wall time, finished or not.

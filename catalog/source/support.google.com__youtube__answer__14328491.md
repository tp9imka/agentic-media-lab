## Fit notes

Fit: high. Level: intro. Maturity: -.

Disclosure is needed for realistic content and AI music, not for clearly fantastical or animated content or for script, caption and thumbnail help. The page says disclosure alone does not limit reach or monetisation, but repeated non-disclosure can lead to removal or YPP suspension. If your generator embeds C2PA, expect a label you cannot turn off. The API equivalent is status.containsSyntheticMedia (revision history record). Title read here: 'Disclosing use of GenAI content'.

## Claims

1. Creators must disclose realistic AI-generated or altered content.

   > We require creators to disclose AI-generated or altered content that seems realistic.

   Locator: Disclosing use of GenAI content, intro. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. YouTube may label content automatically when it carries C2PA metadata, and that label cannot be changed by the creator.

   > However, content made with YouTube’s AI tools, content containing C2PA metadata, or content labeled after manual review can not be adjusted.

   Locator: Automatic detection of AI content. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

3. Repeated failure to disclose can bring a forced label, removal or YouTube Partner Program suspension.

   > Creators who consistently choose not to disclose this information may be subject to manual application of a label, or penalties from YouTube, including removal of content or suspension from the YouTube Partner Program.

   Locator: Risks of not disclosing. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

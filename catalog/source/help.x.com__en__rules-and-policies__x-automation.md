Automated posts for entertainment, informational or novelty purposes are allowed if they comply with all other rules. Duplicate or near-duplicate posts are not, so vary each post. Scripting the website, for example with a browser agent, risks permanent suspension; use the API. The saved text shows the X logo as '>x<' in place of the word X, so quotes avoid those sentences.

## Claims

1. Automated posts for entertainment, information or novelty are allowed if other rules are met.

   > Other automated posts (excluding mentions or replies): Provided you comply with all other rules, you may post automated posts for entertainment, informational, or novelty purposes.

   Locator: Automated posts, 1. Posting automated posts. Source: <https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. Duplicate or substantially similar posts are not allowed, on one account or several.

   > Multiple posts/accounts: You may not post duplicative or substantially similar posts on one account or over multiple accounts you operate.

   Locator: Ground rules, spam. Source: <https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

3. Non-API automation such as scripting the website can lead to permanent suspension.

   > The use of these techniques may result in the permanent suspension of your account.

   Locator: Do not, non-API-based forms of automation. Source: <https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

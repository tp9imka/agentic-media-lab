# YouTube Help - Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts

`source:support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877` - source - <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877>

## Fit notes

Fit: medium. Level: intro. Maturity: -.

Shorts classification is automatic from aspect ratio, length and upload date (with a later cutoff for Official Artist Channels), so the API upload needs no Shorts flag. A Short over one minute with any active copyright claim is blocked worldwide, so generated music must be clean. No resolution or bitrate figures on this page.

## Claims

1. On a standard channel, a square or vertical upload up to three minutes made on or after 2024-10-15 is categorised as a Short (from 2025-12-08 for Official Artist Channels).

   > Any videos uploaded on or after this date with a square or vertical aspect ratio up to three minutes in length will be categorized as Shorts on YouTube.

   Locator: Eligibility for three-minute Shorts. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. A Short over one minute with an active copyright claim is blocked globally.

   > Any Short that is over one minute in duration with an active copyright claim of any type, including manual claims, will be blocked globally on YouTube.

   Locator: Third-party claimed content for Short that is over one minute. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

## Claims

1. Keep text and logos out of the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% on each side.

   > Consider leaving at least 14% of the top, 35% of the bottom, and 6% on each side of your asset free from text, logos, or other important creative elements.

   Locator: Design Recommendations, safe zone paragraph. Source: <https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. Encode H.264, square pixels, fixed frame rate, progressive, stereo AAC at 128 kbps or more.

   > Video Settings: H.264 compression, square pixels, fixed frame rate, progressive scan and stereo AAC audio compression at 128kbps+

   Locator: Design Recommendations. Source: <https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

3. Reels ads must not use files with edit lists or special container boxes.

   > Videos that contain edit lists or special boxes in file containers.

   Locator: Reels ads should not contain, list. Source: <https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

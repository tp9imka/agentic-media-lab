# VBench-2.0: benchmark suite for intrinsic faithfulness

`source:arxiv.org/abs/2503.21755` - source - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21755>

The second VBench suite: it scores single-prompt videos on human fidelity, controllability, creativity, physics and commonsense, using VLMs, LLMs and specialist detectors checked against human labels.

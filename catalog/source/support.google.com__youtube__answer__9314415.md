## Fit notes

Fit: medium. Level: intro. Maturity: -.

Under-60-second Shorts do not get the highlighted moments (top moments, spikes, dips), so a Shorts feedback loop needs the plain retention curve from the Analytics API instead. YouTube publishes no hook length for Shorts on this page; the page does not scope the 30-second intro window by video length. API access was not checked.

## Claims

1. Intro retention is the share of viewers still watching after 30 seconds.

   > Intro tells you what percentage of your audience still watched your video after the first 30 seconds.

   Locator: section Intros. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. Key moments only appear for videos of at least 60 seconds with at least 100 views.

   > Your video should also be at least 60 seconds long and have at least 100 views.

   Locator: note under the moment types. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

3. YouTube suggests moving compelling content earlier because audiences shrink over a video.

   > consider introducing the compelling content earlier in the video—audience sizes typically decrease over the length of the video.

   Locator: section Top moments, recommendations. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

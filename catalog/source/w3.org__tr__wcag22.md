# Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2

`source:w3.org/tr/wcag22` - source - <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>

The W3C Recommendation for accessible web content. Its success criteria cover captions for prerecorded media (1.2.2), text contrast (1.4.3) and flashing (2.3.1).

Concepts: [Visual design](../../docs/glossary.md#visual-design), [Audio and captions](../../docs/glossary.md#audio-and-captions)

## Fit notes

Fit: high. Level: intro. Maturity: -.

Contrast is defined for text on a solid colour. Over moving video, test the caption colour against the lightest and darkest pixels behind it on sampled frames, or put the text on a plate. Large-scale text means at least 18 point, or 14 point bold. The flash rule matters for glitch and strobe transitions: count luminance flips per second. Written for web pages, applied here to video by analogy.

## Claims

1. Text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background.

   > The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, except for the following:

   Locator: SC 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), Level AA. Source: <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. Large-scale text may drop to 3:1.

   > Large-scale text and images of large-scale text have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1;

   Locator: SC 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), first exception. Source: <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

3. Nothing may flash more than three times in any one second, unless below the flash thresholds.

   > Web pages do not contain anything that flashes more than three times in any one second period, or the flash is below the general flash and red flash thresholds.

   Locator: SC 2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below Threshold, Level A. Source: <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

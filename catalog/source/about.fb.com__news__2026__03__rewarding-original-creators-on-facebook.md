## Claims

1. Stitching clips together or narrating what is already on screen without adding anything meaningful counts as unoriginal and is likely to be deprioritised.

   > Simply watching along, reacting with facial expressions, stitching multiple clips together, or narrating what’s already on screen — without adding anything meaningful — is considered unoriginal and will likely be deprioritized in Feed and Reels.

   Locator: section Clearer Guidelines for Original Content, bullet 2. Source: <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. Minor edits to another creator's post, such as adding captions or changing speed, are also unoriginal.

   > making low-value changes to someone else’s content like adding borders, inserting captions, and changing the reel’s speed,

   Locator: section Clearer Guidelines for Original Content, bullet 3. Source: <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

3. Accounts that keep posting mainly unoriginal content may be made non-recommendable and demonetised.

   > they may see their account deemed non-recommendable and demonetized, if they continue to post primarily unoriginal content.

   Locator: section Clearer Guidelines for Original Content, paragraph after the bullets. Source: <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

# YouTube channel monetization policies (inauthentic content)

YouTube's Partner Program policy page. Its inauthentic-content section, renamed from 'repetitious content' on July 15, 2025, sets the bar for AI-assisted and templated channels.

## Fit notes

Fit: high. Level: intro. Maturity: -.

AI use is allowed and named as monetisable when it serves your own narrative; templated, interchangeable videos are not. Reused-content review applies to the whole channel, so a run of near-identical generated Shorts can cost monetisation on everything. Separate rule: AI personas giving health, legal, finance or political advice cannot monetise. Policy text, not a measured threshold; reviewers judge.

## Claims

1. Monetised content must not be mass-produced, generic or repetitive.

   > Not be mass-produced, generic, repetitive, or manipulative. It should be made for the enjoyment or education of viewers, rather than for the sole purpose of getting views.

   Locator: Inauthentic content. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. AI content from generic templates that looks mass-produced, without the creator's own insight, is a named example of inauthentic content.

   > AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production without adding the creator’s original, authentic insights or perspective

   Locator: Generic or Repetitive Content, examples. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

3. Using AI to visualise a character and story you invented is a named example of content that can monetise.

   > Content that expresses your unique creative voice, like using AI to visualize a unique character and narrative you invented

   Locator: Examples of what is allowed to monetize. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

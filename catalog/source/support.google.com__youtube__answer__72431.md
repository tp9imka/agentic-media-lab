## Fit notes

Fit: high. Level: intro. Maturity: -.

Render 3840x2160 (or 2160x3840 for Shorts) and export JPG or PNG under 2 MB if the upload may come from a phone; desktop allows 50 MB. Many guides still say 1280x720 from memory; this page asks for larger. Keep the subject and text inside a 4:5 centre crop when the video is vertical. Custom thumbnails need a verified account. A thumbnail that breaks Community Guidelines can earn a strike. Limits change; re-check before a launch.

## Claims

1. YouTube recommends 3840x2160 for video thumbnails and 2160x3840 for Shorts, with a 640 px minimum.

   > Have a resolution of 3840 x 2160 pixels for videos and 2160 x 3840 for Shorts, with a minimum width of 640 pixels for videos and a minimum height of 640 pixels for Shorts.

   Locator: Custom thumbnail best practices, Image size & resolution. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

2. The upload limit is 2 MB from mobile and 50 MB from desktop.

   > Mobile: 2 MB for video thumbnails or 10 MB for podcasts. Desktop: 50MB for video, Shorts, and podcast thumbnails.

   Locator: Custom thumbnail best practices, Image size & resolution. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431>, retrieved 2026-11-12.

3. A 16:9 custom thumbnail on a vertical video is replaced by an auto-generated 4:5 one on home, explore and subscription pages.

   > Note: Vertical videos with 16:9 custom thumbnails will be replaced by an auto-generated 4:5 thumbnail on the home, explore, and subscription pages.

   Locator: Custom thumbnail best practices, note under aspect ratios. Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431>, retrieved 2026-11-12.
